The car trailer container is a purpose-built special purpose vehicle designed for commercial and industrial applications. Export models from Chinese manufacturers typically feature proven chassis options including Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW, ISUZU and MAN, with factory-direct FOB pricing that runs 30-40% below local dealer prices in most destination markets.

Applications for the car trailer container span municipal services, construction sites, industrial facilities and agricultural operations. Latin American buyers increasingly source directly from Chinese factories to bypass import markup layers.

Pricing for a car trailer container varies significantly based on chassis origin, body capacity, and specification level.
