# Reefer Van Small - Technical Overview and Buying Tips

> 冷藏 厢式 小型

When sourcing a reefer van small from China, buyers should focus on three key areas: chassis reliability, body construction quality, and after-sales parts availability. Chinese export builds pair proven engines (Cummins, Weichai, ISUZU-derived units) with inexpensive spare parts networks in every major African and Asian port city, making them the dominant choice for fleet operators.

Pricing for a reefer van small varies significantly based on chassis origin, body capacity, and specification level. Chinese chassis builds offer the best value, while Japanese (ISUZU, Hino) and European (MAN, Volvo, Scania) options command premiums of 25% to 65%. Ocean freight by RO-RO or container adds $3,000 to $8,000 depending on destination port.

The reefer van small is a purpose-built special purpose vehicle designed for commercial and industrial applications. Export models from Chinese manufacturers typically feature proven chassis options including Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW, ISUZU and MAN, with factory-direct FOB pricing that runs 30-40% below local dealer prices in most destination markets.

Proper maintenance of a reefer van small centers on the PTO engagement system, hydraulic oil quality, and body mounting integrity. Most Chinese export models are designed for simple field maintenance with standard tools. A complete spare parts kit (filters, seals, hydraulic hoses) should be ordered with the initial purchase to ensure availability in remote operating locations.

For buyers importing a reefer van small from China, the recommended process is: (1) obtain formal quotations from at least three verified manufacturers, (2) arrange third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, BV, or TUV), (3) use 30% T/T deposit with balance against shipping documents, and (4) confirm written warranty terms including claim response times. This keeps risk low and ensures a clean paper trail for customs clearance.
