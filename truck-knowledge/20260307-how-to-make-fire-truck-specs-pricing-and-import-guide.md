This keeps risk low and ensures a clean paper trail for customs clearance.

## FAQ

**Q: what is a semi truck cab card**

A semi truck cab card is a document that lists the vehicle’s registered specifications and operating authority details. It typically includes information such as the truck’s VIN, license plate, registered weight, axle configuration, carrier name, and sometimes the states or jurisdictions where the truck is authorized to operate. For commercial fleets, the cab card is important for compliance checks and roadside inspections because it helps verify that the truck is properly registered for its intended use. Depending on the state or country, the exact format and required details may vary. If you need, I can also explain how a cab card differs from a title, registration, or DOT documents.

**Q: what does the red and blue lights mean on a police car**

On a police car, red and blue lights are emergency warning lights used to alert drivers and pedestrians that the vehicle is responding to an incident or performing an official law-enforcement duty. The colors are highly visible and signal that others should slow down, yield, and follow local traffic laws.
