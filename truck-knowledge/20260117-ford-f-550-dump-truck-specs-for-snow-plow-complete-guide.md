## FAQ

**Q: what is a car carrier truck called**

A car carrier truck is commonly called a **car carrier**, **auto transporter**, or **vehicle transport truck**. In the trucking and logistics industry, it is often referred to as a **car hauler**. These trucks are designed to transport multiple vehicles at once, usually on a specialized trailer with one or two levels. For single-vehicle transport, some people may also use the term **rollback tow truck** or **flatbed tow truck**, depending on the design. If you are sourcing this type of truck for commercial use, the exact name can vary by configuration and capacity, but “car carrier truck” and “auto transporter” are the most widely used terms.

**Q: how to choose high quality snow removal truck**

To choose a high-quality snow removal truck, first match the truck size and engine power to your snowfall volume, road type, and operating hours. Check the durability of the chassis, transmission, and hydraulic system, since these parts face heavy loads. Look for corrosion-resistant materials, especially for the body, plow frame, and spreader components. Confirm the truck supports your required attachments, such as plows, salt spreaders, or blowers. Also evaluate fuel efficiency, service access, spare parts availability, and after-sales support. A reliable supplier should provide technical specifications, warranty terms, and maintenance guidance. If possible, inspect real working cases or request a demo before purchase.
