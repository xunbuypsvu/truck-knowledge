## Field Notes

For reference, the export price of a standard curtain side box body truck from China usually ranges from USD 18,000 to USD 42,000. A refrigerated curtain side truck generally costs more, with common export prices between USD 32,000 and USD 75,000, depending on chassis brand, refrigeration performance, insulation thickness, and regional compliance needs.

Curtain side trucks are used in a wide range of applications. In box body form, they are ideal for general cargo, retail distribution, furniture, electronics, and household goods. In refrigerated form, they are widely used for meat, seafood, dairy, frozen foods, flowers, and vaccines or medical supplies that require controlled temperatures. Their side curtain design allows forklifts to load from multiple angles, which reduces turnaround time and improves warehouse efficiency.
