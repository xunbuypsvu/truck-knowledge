## Field Notes

Before placing an order, compare quotations using the same technical basis: tank volume, chassis model, engine power, pump brand, spray system, and warranty terms. This makes it easier to judge true value and avoid hidden upgrades later. For overseas importers, the best purchase is not simply the cheapest or the largest. It is the **water tanker truck** that matches local roads, daily workload, and long-term service requirements.

If your work is light to medium duty, a standard 4x2 truck with a 150-220 hp engine may be enough. For more intensive municipal use, choose a stronger chassis with 240-350 hp and a larger water pump. Buyers who need maximum uptime should pay attention to axle rating, tire size, and frame reinforcement, not only tank volume.
