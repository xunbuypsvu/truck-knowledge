## Field Notes

Buyers importing from China often compare only the FOB price, but the real cost is in ownership. A lower-priced boom lift truck may look attractive at first, yet maintenance, fuel or electricity use, tire wear, and after-sales parts can change the economics quickly. For example, a diesel model may cost less upfront but consume more over a three-year fleet cycle, while an electric unit may have a higher battery replacement cost after several years.

Useful comparison points include:
1. Working height versus horizontal outreach.
2. Basket load versus actual tool weight and operator count.
3. Chassis brand, axle strength, and local parts support.
4. Stabilizer type and setup speed on uneven ground.
