# Tractor Truck With Crane for International Buyers

> 牵引车 吊机

The tractor truck with crane is typically configured with a power take-off (PTO) system driving the specialized equipment. Engine options range from 180 to 420 hp depending on capacity requirements, with 6-speed to 12-speed manual or AMT gearboxes. Emission standards range from Euro 3 to Euro 6 depending on the destination market's requirements.

For buyers importing a tractor truck with crane from China, the recommended process is: (1) obtain formal quotations from at least three verified manufacturers, (2) arrange third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, BV, or TUV), (3) use 30% T/T deposit with balance against shipping documents, and (4) confirm written warranty terms including claim response times. This keeps risk low and ensures a clean paper trail for customs clearance.

Applications for the tractor truck with crane span municipal services, construction sites, industrial facilities and agricultural operations. In African markets, government and NGO procurement drives significant demand, while in Southeast Asia the private construction sector is the primary buyer. Latin American buyers increasingly source directly from Chinese factories to bypass import markup layers.

## Field Notes

Container shipping is suitable for smaller tractor truck components, CKD kits, or when the vehicle can be partially disassembled. A 40-foot flat rack or open-top container may be used for some special vehicle exports, but it requires careful loading and strong fixation. Bulk shipping is usually chosen for oversized or project cargo when RO-RO space is limited. It can be more economical for certain routes, although the truck may experience more handling during loading and unloading.

For overseas buyers, the best shipping method depends on budget, destination port, and delivery urgency. Before shipment, confirm chassis dimensions, gross weight, and port handling rules. A reliable China supplier will coordinate factory inspection, customs filing, freight booking, and document release to ensure the tractor truck arrives ready for registration and use.
