When sourcing a FOTON 4x2 box truck for sale from China, buyers should focus on three key areas: chassis reliability, body construction quality, and after-sales parts availability. Proper maintenance of a FOTON 4x2 box truck for sale centers on the PTO engagement system, hydraulic oil quality, and body mounting integrity.
