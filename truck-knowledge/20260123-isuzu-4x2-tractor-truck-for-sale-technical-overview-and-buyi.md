**Q: what does a tanker truck hold**

A tanker truck is designed to carry and transport liquids or gases in bulk. Depending on its configuration and industry use, it may hold fuel such as gasoline or diesel, water, chemicals, milk, liquid food products, or industrial gases. Some tanker trucks are also built for hazardous materials and must meet strict safety and regulatory standards. The exact capacity varies by truck size and tank design, but many tanker trucks can carry anywhere from a few thousand to over 10,000 gallons. If you need a specific type of tanker, the load capacity and material compatibility should be matched to the product being transported.
