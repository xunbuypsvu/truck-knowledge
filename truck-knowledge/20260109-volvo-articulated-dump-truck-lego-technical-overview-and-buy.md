**Q: how big are mining dump trucks**

Mining dump trucks are among the largest haul vehicles in the world. Their size varies by model, but rigid-frame mining dump trucks are typically about 25 to 30 feet long, 15 to 20 feet wide, and 15 to 25 feet tall. The largest ultra-class trucks can carry more than 400 tons and may be over 30 feet wide with tire diameters exceeding 13 feet. Payload capacity is the key measure, and it usually ranges from around 100 tons to 400+ tons depending on the application.
